            How rewards work
            • Ticket Closed development ticket: 100 XP, 1 treat
            • Help Desk Resolved HD issue: 200 XP, 2 treats
            • Critical Critical production issue (HD, severity 3+): 400 XP, 3 treats
            • Bonus Challenges pay extra XP and treats: busy days, quick HD fixes, full weeks, new requesters, and a few secrets.
            • Ticket and GitHub XP increase your overall level and shared evolution credits. Choose Robot, Cat, Dog, Dragon or Slime freely in Customize. Buy stages 2–5 for 5,000 / 15,000 / 30,000 / 50,000 credits per buddy. Spending never lowers your level; earned cosmetics and care are shared. Display any owned stage, or reset every evolution for a full spending refund. XP corrections keep owned stages but may create a credit deficit.
            • GitHub Verified contributions and challenges earn XP. Opening, merging, reviewing, and helpful feedback have distinct rewards, subject to configured limits. Refreshing does not award the same activity twice.
            • A clean merge requires review evidence, not silence. Missing or incomplete review data is marked unknown. Milestone cosmetics appear in Customize and stay unlocked.
            • Only closed tickets assigned to you count, each exactly once. A reopened ticket's XP is held until it closes again; unlocked items are always kept.
